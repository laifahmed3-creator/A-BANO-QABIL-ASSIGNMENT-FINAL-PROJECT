OUR FOUNDER

Asghar Ali

From Professional Supervisor to Founder

Asghar Ali was a retired supervisor who spent his professional career working on construction and engineering-related projects.

During his career, he was involved in major building and construction projects in Karachi. His experience as a supervisor gave him practical knowledge of construction work, project management, teamwork and the responsibilities involved in completing large projects.

THE BEGINNING OF THE COMPANY

Asghar Ali Engineering and Works

After completing his professional career as a supervisor, Asghar Ali established Asghar Ali Engineering and Works.

The company represents the next chapter of his professional journey, bringing together his experience, practical knowledge and understanding of engineering and construction work.

The foundation of Asghar Ali Engineering and Works reflects his transition from working on construction projects to establishing his own engineering and works business.

HIS LEGACY

Experience That Became a Foundation

Asghar Ali's professional experience, his work as a supervisor and his involvement in major construction projects became an important part of the foundation of Asghar Ali Engineering and Works.

His story continues through the work and management of the company.
